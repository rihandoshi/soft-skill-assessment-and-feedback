# Stage 7: MIT validation of the audio categories

Generated by `scripts/explainability/validate_categories_mit.py`. MIT Interview Dataset: 138 mock interviews, 69 MIT undergraduates, each interviewed before (`P#`) and after (`PP#`) coaching, rated per interview by the same 9 Turkers. Nothing is fitted on MIT: pipeline, references, thresholds and gate are RecruitView's.

## 1. Data

- Interviews transcribed and aligned: 138 of 138; median share of ASR words given a speaker 99%, of transcript words matched 86%.
- All five questions found in 96% of interviews; answers cut: 674 (median 46 s); interviews with labels and answers: 138.
- **Recording quality:** MIT is a far-field room microphone. Median speech level -42 dBFS, SNR 12 dB, SQUIM PESQ 1.21; the RecruitView acoustic gate would fail **91%** of MIT answers. The gate is a feedback rule, so it is not applied here: recording conditions are the same for every interview, and every test is repeated with the quality composite partialled out.
