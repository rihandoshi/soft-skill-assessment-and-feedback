# Stage 4: verbatim ASR and word-level descriptors

Generated by `scripts/explainability/extract_word_level_descriptors.py` (ASR: `run_verbatim_asr.py`, faster-whisper medium.en, word timestamps, filler-preserving prompt). Same measurement code as Stage 1; only the words and the sentence boundaries differ. Statistics on unique recordings unless stated.

## 1. ASR coverage and quality

- Clips with ASR words: 1984 / 2011; words: 143,025; filled pauses: 2,609 (1.8% of tokens).
- Words removed as possible hallucinations (no VAD speech within 0.1 s): 697 in 247 clips.
- Possible repetition loops: 10 clips (listed in `tables/asr_checks.csv`).
- ASR / transcript word ratio: median 1.00, p5 0.92, p95 1.12; 38 unique clips outside [0.7, 1.4] (`tables/asr_checks.csv`).
- Median word probability: 0.912.

## 2. Sentences: transcript lines vs real sentences

- Transcript lines (Stage 1): 8,857; ASR sentences: 7,450.
- Median duration: transcript line 5.6 s, ASR sentence 6.1 s (p95 18.2 s).
- Only **24%** of transcript line ends fall within 0.5 s of a real sentence end (6,858 line ends checked). The rest are subtitle breaks inside a sentence, where Stage 1 measured trailing off and terminal rises anyway.

### Terminal pitch at real sentence ends

- Last sentence of each answer (a real end in both stages): median terminal slope Stage 1 -4.34 st/s, Stage 4 -4.32 st/s, Spearman 0.88: consistent.
- Sentence ends **inside** an answer: median +0.25 st/s (rising). In spontaneous speech a sentence that is not the end of the turn usually ends with a continuation rise ("...and then"). A terminal rise therefore does not mean uptalk; `uptalk` stays experimental and Stage 8 should label rising *statements* separately from continuation rises.
- End-of-sentence energy drop is larger at real ends (median -3.9 dB vs -1.5 dB at transcript line breaks): the voice normally falls at a sentence end, so the `trails_off` reference must be (and is) refitted on Stage 4 sentences.

## 5. Filled pauses (for the text branch)

- Answers with at least one filled pause: 45%; median rate 0.00/min, p90 8.80/min.
- Whisper under-transcribes fillers even with the prompt; recall has not been hand-checked. Filler counting, the filler hand-check (precision/recall on 30 clips) and filler feedback are the text branch's job; this branch uses filled pauses only to keep them out of the word counts.
