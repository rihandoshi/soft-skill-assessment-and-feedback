## 2. Does SER describe the moment or the person?

ICC(1) = share of answer-level variance that is between speakers (Stage 3 found 0.80-0.85 for jitter / shimmer, 0.97 for absolute pitch). Window-level: correlation of adjacent windows (smoothness of the curve).

| Measure | ICC (speaker share) | adjacent-window Spearman |
|---|---|---|
| `ser_arousal_mean` | 0.87 | 0.90 |
| `ser_dominance_mean` | 0.87 | 0.89 |
| `ser_valence_mean` | 0.40 | 0.61 |

**Are the three outputs different measurements?** Spearman across speech windows: arousal-dominance **0.98** (speaker-relative z: 0.94), arousal-valence 0.52, dominance-valence 0.53. Arousal and dominance are practically one dimension here: vocal activation (energetic / forceful vs flat). MSP-Podcast listeners' own arousal and dominance ratings are strongly correlated, and the model inherits that. So the measure can say 'flatter / less forceful than your usual', not 'less confident' as something separate from energy, and `tension_rise` cannot tell tension from enthusiasm.
