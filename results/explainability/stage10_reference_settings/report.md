# Stage 10 (Phase 2): one reference per recording setting

Generated by `compare_settings.py`. `recorded_answer` = RecruitView answers compared with RecruitView train answers (unique recordings). `live_interview` = each MIT student's answers compared with the other 68 students' answers (both of a student's interviews are left out together). New live recordings use all 69 students (`level_cuts.json`).

## 1. Which components shift between settings

Each live-interview answer's percentile among recorded answers (tie-aware, as in the levels). Median percentile: 50 = no shift. Tail shares: answers at or below the 20th / at or above the 80th percentile, next to the same shares within the recorded answers themselves (not always 20%: many recorded answers tie, e.g. 80% have no long mid-sentence pause). A component keeps one reference only if the median percentile is within 40-60 and both live tail shares are within 5 points of the recorded ones (fixed in advance).

| filled-pause score (vocametrix) (`p_interjection`) | yes | 0.0834 | 0.264 | 82 | 20% / 2% | 20% / 53% | needs its own reference |
