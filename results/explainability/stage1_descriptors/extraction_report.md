## Deviations from the stage guide

- **Measurement / comparison / feedback are kept strictly separate.** The guide's step 5 'ratio to the train median for the same question type' is a comparison against fitted reference data, so it moves to Stage 2 with the other train-fitted reference tables. Stage 1 outputs raw duration and word count only.
- **Descriptor status** (active / experimental / diagnostic) is recorded in `descriptor_registry.json`. Per Stage 0 and review: loudness is diagnostic only; pitch-based measures, trailing off and voice quality are experimental (measured, normed and validated, but not used for feedback yet).
- **Pauses are reported as classified rates and ratios**, not raw counts (Stage 0: raw counts track answer length). The raw count is kept as a diagnostic.
- **Sentence windows are VAD-trimmed** inside the timestamp window [a, b+1), which gives ~0.1 s precision for durations instead of 1 s.
- **Articulation rate** is reported both as syllables/s (standard unit; heuristic syllable counter) and words/min of speech time (easier to phrase in feedback).
- Jitter and shimmer (answer level) were added as experimental composure stress proxies for Stage 5.
- **Fixes found by inspecting the extremes** (first run): (1) isolated VAD blips (< 0.3 s, >= 1 s silence both sides) are removed, since they stretched sentence spans and turned post-answer silence into fake mid-sentence pauses; (2) per-sentence rates need >= 5 words, and lines implying > 8 syllables/s (garbled ASR text in a short window) are marked `timing_suspect` and their rates withheld; (3) digital-silence frames (recording dropouts, -300 dB) are excluded from all intensity statistics and counted in `dropout_frac`, and trailing off compares syllable-peak level (p80) rather than mean dB; (4) the terminal F0 slope uses the last voiced run of >= 50 ms, octave-folds frames > 9 st from the sentence median, ignores weak voicing > 25 dB below the syllable peaks and fits Theil-Sen; (5) a pause is mid-sentence only if it lies inside a sentence's VAD-trimmed speech.
- **Pitch range rule:** De Looze & Hirst's quartile rule (floor 0.75 x Q1, ceiling 1.5 x Q3), bounded to 0.6-1.8 x the speaker median so halving/doubling errors fall outside the range, instead of the guide's q15/q85, because pass-1 octave errors contaminate q85 for some speakers.
