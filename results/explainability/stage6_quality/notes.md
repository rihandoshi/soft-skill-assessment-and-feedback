# Stage 6 notes: recording quality

Hand-written interpretation; `report.md` is generated by `fit_quality_gate.py`.

## What was done

- `run_quality_scores.py`: torchaudio `SQUIM_OBJECTIVE` (estimated STOI / PESQ / SI-SDR, no clean reference) on 10-s
  speech chunks, plus signal checks (VAD SNR, noise floor, clipping, level). All 1,993 clips, a few minutes on GPU.
- `fit_quality_gate.py`: quality composite = mean train percentile of PESQ, STOI and VAD SNR; gate at train p10.
- `build_evidence.py`: `quality_gate` v1 in the evidence JSON.

## Findings

1. **Quality is mostly a property of the speaker's setup** (ICC 0.78 PESQ, 0.83 SNR). RecruitView's `video_quality`
   label says little about audio (rho ~0.14; "Low" video recordings actually have *better* audio on average).
2. **Loudness: mostly a recording artefact.** Our speech level (dBFS) relates to the scores between people
   (rho -0.27 confidence), but after regressing out the quality measures this falls to -0.06 (not significant);
   within a person every loudness measure is ~0 after adjustment (Stage 0's VAD RMS: -0.08 -> -0.02). Exception:
   the legacy VAD mean RMS keeps a between-person relation (-0.49 after adjustment); it is a different level
   measure (mean linear RMS over VAD regions) and not used for feedback. **Conclusion: no loudness feedback.**
3. **Raters score cleaner recordings slightly lower** between people (PESQ rho -0.29), not within a person.
   Probably setup / population (same direction as "calmer speakers score higher"), not a delivery cue. A rating
   confound to remember for Stage 7.
4. **Which descriptors quality distorts:** jitter / shimmer strongly (between rho -0.65; why they were dropped as
   composure proxies), intensity variability (+0.59 between, +0.19 within), SER arousal (+0.45 between, ~0 within:
   the speaker-relative use is safe). Pitch, end-drop and terminal slope are barely affected.
5. **Flags on low-quality audio:** `monotone` fires 2.2x more often below the gate (gated), `hesitation` 1.7x and
   `slow_start` 1.3x (not gated: timing flags; mid-pause rate also moves with quality within a person, +0.20).
   `trails_off` is unaffected (its own noise guard works).

## The gate

- **basic** (unchanged v0): no speech / transcript timing suspect / > 1% dropouts -> no delivery feedback.
- **acoustic**: composite < train p10 or > 0.1% clipped samples -> expressiveness, projection, composure and
  assertiveness and their flags are withheld; pace, pausing and conciseness stay. Fails on 11% of train / val,
  13% of test; 13 of 331 speakers have every answer gated.

## Still open

- `hesitation`: stays experimental and is still computed, but is **provisionally withheld below the gate**
  (`provisional_flags` in `quality_gate.json`). Not removed: Stage 7 (MIT) / Stage 8 (listening) decide whether the
  extra firings on poor audio are real hesitation or a VAD artefact.
- SQUIM was trained on simulated noise (DNS 2020); its scores are a ranking aid here, not calibrated MOS.
