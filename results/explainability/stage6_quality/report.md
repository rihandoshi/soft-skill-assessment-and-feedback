## 4. The gate

- **acoustic**: quality composite >= **16.3** (train p10) and clipping <= 1.00e-03 (fixed). Fails -> categories expressiveness, projection, composure, assertiveness and flags monotone, trails_off, uptalk, low_dominance, tension_rise, composure_drop are withheld; pace / pausing / conciseness stay. Provisionally also withheld (still computed, not removed): hesitation.
- **basic** (v0, unchanged): speech present, transcript timing OK, dropouts <= 1%. Fails -> no delivery feedback.
- Acoustic gate failure rate: test 13.4%, train 11.1%, val 11.3%.
- By RecruitView `video_quality`: High 12.5%, Low 6.3%.
- Speakers with every answer gated: 13 of 331; with some but not all: 70.

Written to `quality_gate.json`; `build_evidence.py` applies it (`quality_gate` field, withheld flags listed).
