# Stage 2: Reference distributions, flags and scores

Descriptor tables: **asr** (`results/explainability/stage4_asr/tables/segment_descriptors.csv`).

Generated by `scripts/explainability/fit_reference_distributions.py` (`stage2-v0`). References fitted on **train participants only, unique recordings only**: 1357 answers from 231 participants, 5062 sentences, 4808 mid-sentence pauses. Val/test are only compared against them.

At inference, `delivery_reference.py` + `percentile_tables.json` are all that is needed: a new answer's measurements are compared with the stored quantiles, and with the median of the same speaker's other answers in that interview. No training data is loaded.

## Flag firing rates (% of unique answers)

Target for active flags: 5-15% of answers (0% = threshold wrong, ~40% = not informative). Thresholds start from the stage guide; `too_fast` and `long_answer` were recalibrated on the **train** firing rate only (grid: p90/p95/p97 x +20/25/30%, and 1.75/2/2.25/2.5 x median). Val/test are reported as a check and were not used for tuning.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `composure_drop` | experimental | composure | 9.4 | 8.7 | 11.3 |  | the smoothed dominance curve falls by > 1 speaker SD within the answer AND the fall is above p90 of comparable answers (same length bucket: longer answers have more windows and larger maximum falls; > 1 SD alone fired on 70%); timestamp = from the peak to the low point |
| `hesitation` | experimental | pausing | 9.2 | 9.1 | 11.7 |  | longest mid-sentence pause >= max(1.0 s, p90 of comparable answers' longest mid-sentence pause) AND not shorter than the speaker's usual longest mid-sentence pause. ## Category profile: one hard class per audio soft-skill category

Every answer gets one class in each of the seven categories of EXPLAINABILITY_README §3. Category index = mean of its signals' percentiles (oriented toward the 'high' pole) among comparable train answers; classes cut at the train p15 / p85 of the index (unique train recordings). `vs your usual` compares the index with the speaker's other answers (same cut points on the train distribution of that difference). No model is trained; only distributions and cut points are estimated. Only categories with `use_for_feedback` drive advice; the rest are reported (dashboard, temporal model, validation) until MIT / annotation support them.

**Consistency with the flags** (share of flagged answers that are in the matching 'high' class):

Flags and classes are complementary: a class describes the whole answer against other people; a flag marks a specific moment that is unusual against other people **and** the speaker's own usual (with a timestamp and the quoted sentence for the feedback).

## Category scores (dashboard only)

## Decisions carried over from Stages 0-1

- **Only active descriptors produce flags, scores and evidence.** Experimental ones (pitch, trailing off, uptalk, latency) get reference tables and flags in `flags.csv` for Stage 3 validation, but never reach the LLM.
- **`too_slow` is experimental** (Stage 4: with verbatim ASR the slow tail is real speech; still no rater support, so it is computed for validation but never reaches the LLM). Pace scoring stays one-sided.
- **Pause ratio and between-sentence pauses are normed but neither scored nor flagged**: in Stage 0, more silence went with *higher* scores, even within a participant.
- **Loudness and absolute pitch have no reference tables at all** (diagnostic: they identify the speaker/microphone).
- **Duplicates** are excluded from fitting, and from each other's speaker baseline.
- **Stage 3 (RecruitView validation)**: `hesitation` and `rushed_opening` moved to experimental (no within-participant relation; raters favour *more* pausing between people); the pausing category left the dashboard scores and strengths; `too_fast` and `long_answer` stay active with a `rater_support` note that travels into the evidence JSON so the feedback can be worded descriptively. EBM optimal bands are stored as metadata (`stage3` in each reference) and only replace the provisional percentile band when Stage 3 found them identified. See `results/explainability/stage3_recruitview_validation/report.md`.
